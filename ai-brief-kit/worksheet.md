### 1. What it does

This __________ takes __________ and gives __________, so __________ can __________.

- [ ] No technology names your listener wouldn't use

### 2. The honest promise

It __________, but it can __________, so __________.

- [ ] One real limit
- [ ] What you do about that limit

### 3. Who holds the wheel

For __________, __________ (named role) checks / decides before __________.

*(Optional)* For __________, it runs on its own.

- [ ] A named role, not "a human"
- [ ] Passes the test: if it's wrong, who gets hurt, and can we undo it?

### 4. The ask

I need __________ from __________ by __________.

- [ ] One decision they can say yes or no to
- [ ] An owner
- [ ] A date

---

### Final check

- [ ] Under 30 seconds out loud
- [ ] Could your listener repeat sentence 1 to someone else?
- [ ] Nothing promised that isn't true
