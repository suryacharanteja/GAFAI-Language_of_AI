# Case: The Procedures Assistant

The case used in the workshop. It's based on a real type of project, simplified and anonymised.

[Back to the kit](README.md)

---

## The situation

A large operations company has thousands of pages of procedures covering safety, operations and HR. Staff often can't find the right page, so they ask their supervisors, who end up answering the same questions many times a week.

## What the team built

An AI assistant that staff can ask questions in plain language. It:

1. Searches the company's own procedures
2. Writes a short answer
3. Links to the page the answer came from

## What it's good at

- Answers in seconds, at any hour
- Finds the right document even when staff don't know its name
- Shows where every answer came from

## Where it can go wrong

- In testing, about [1 in X] answers had a mistake that sounded confident, for example mixing up two similar procedures or quoting an outdated version.
- It's meant to answer only from the documents it was given, but it can sometimes fill a gap with something that isn't in them.

## Three questions staff might ask it

| # | Question |
|---|---|
| Q1 | "Where's the leave request form?" |
| Q2 | "How many days of compassionate leave do I get?" |
| Q3 | "What are the safety steps before repairing this pump?" |

For each one, decide the level of control: **1** a person decides, **2** a person checks, or **3** it runs alone. Use the test: if it's wrong, who gets hurt, and can we undo it?
